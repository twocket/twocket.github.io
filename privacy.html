Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Loany Poker (for Android and iOS, “the game”) works with, why, and what your rights are. In short: the game has no accounts and keeps your progress on your device. Data leave your device only through the services of Google and Apple that the game uses for ads, purchases and backups.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of personal data is Tomáš Chejn, a sole trader, ID No. (IČO) 11899565, Čsl. dobrovolců 1713, 511 01 Turnov, Czech Republic. E-mail: twocket.developer@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (chips, debt, card album, purchases unlocked in the game, settings) is saved only in the game's storage on your device. We have no servers and we don't receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it (unless it is in a backup, see section 3).

The game doesn't ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, location, photos or any other permission beyond internet access.

3. Backups and cloud saves

  • Google Play Games (Android). If you're signed in to Google Play Games, the game saves a copy of your progress in Saved Games in your Google account, so you can continue on a new phone. Google processes your Play Games player profile for this. You can delete the game's data in the Google Play Games settings.
  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone, Android may back up the game's save to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game.
  • iCloud backup (iOS). If iCloud Backup is turned on, iOS may back up the game's save with your device backup.

These backups are stored in your own Google or Apple account under their terms. We can't access them.

4. Purchases

You can buy cosmetic card decks and the removal of ads. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We never see your payment card or account details. The store tells the game which products you own, and we receive sales reports from the store (for example the order number, product, price, date and country) that we keep for accounting and to handle refunds and support requests.

5. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). For this, the AdMob SDK in the game collects and sends to Google:

  • the device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data about ad performance.

Google uses these data to show ads, to measure them, to prevent fraud and, with your consent, to personalise ads. Players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent when the game starts (Google's consent form). You can change your choice anytime in the game's settings under Privacy options. Without consent, Google shows only non-personalised or limited ads. On iOS, the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) and doesn't access the IDFA if you decline.

The game is not directed at children and tells AdMob so. If you buy No Ads, the game no longer shows interstitial ads; ads you choose to watch for a reward stay optional.

More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy.

6. Store statistics and crash reports

Google Play and the App Store give us aggregated statistics (installs, ratings, crashes and performance). Crash reports reach us only if you allowed your device to share them with app developers. They don't identify you to us.

7. Contacting us

If you write to us, we use your e-mail address and the content of your message only to answer you, and we keep them for as long as needed to resolve your request (usually up to 2 years).

9. Who receives the data

Google (AdMob, Google Play, Google Play Games) and Apple (App Store, iCloud) process the data described above, partly as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. They may transfer data outside the EU, especially to the USA, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. We don't sell your data and don't share it with anyone else.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (in the game under Privacy options). Because we don't store your game data ourselves, the fastest way to delete it is to uninstall the game and delete the game's data in Google Play Games or your backups. Ad data can be reset by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in the device settings. For anything else, write to twocket.developer@gmail.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Czech Republic it is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz).

11. Age

The game contains simulated gambling and is intended only for adults (18+). We don't knowingly process children's data.

12. Changes

We will update this policy when the game changes how it handles data. The date at the top shows the latest version.
