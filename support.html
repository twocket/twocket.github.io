Support

Something wrong, or an idea? Write to twocket.developer@gmail.com. I usually answer within a few days.

When reporting a problem, please include your phone model, its system version, the game version (in your phone's app settings) and what happened just before.

Frequently asked questions

I bought something, but I don't have it in the game.

Open the store in the game and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Google or Apple account you used for the purchase. A payment that is still pending (for example cash at a shop) shows as Pending and the item arrives once the payment is confirmed.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store, so refunds go through them: on Android in Google Play, on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com. After a refund, the refunded item is removed from the game.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my album?

On Android, the game saves your progress in Google Play Games under your Google account. Sign in to Google Play Games with the same account on the new phone and start the game: the Banker will tell you your progress is back. Purchases always come back with Restore purchases.

Progress can't be moved between Android and iPhone.

Can I buy chips or win real money?

No. Chips have no real value and can't be bought, sold or cashed out. When you run out, the Banker lends you more. You can buy only cosmetic card decks and the removal of ads.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the game's settings, tap Privacy options (shown to players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). On iPhone you can also allow or deny tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. You can play without internet. Ads, purchases and the cloud save need a connection.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game; the progress on your device is deleted with it. On Android, you can also delete the game's data saved in Google Play Games in its settings. More in the privacy policy.
